# Shared local design-preview artwork

`brand-*.svg` contains the Apple TV, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch and Xbox paths
from Simple Icons 11.15.0. Geometry is unchanged; the presentation fill is ivory.
Use the files as images on dark backgrounds, or as CSS masks for other colors.
No remote requests are made by the previews.

Exact upstream URLs and original hashes are in `brand-attribution.json`.
The upstream CC0 dedication is retained in `simple-icons-LICENSE.md`.
Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; the mockups imply no
endorsement and use the family mark without inferring a hardware generation.

`symbol-*.svg` are original geometric HDMI, computer, disc and game-controller
symbols for unbranded or unknown devices. They never determine a device from its
physical HDMI port number.
